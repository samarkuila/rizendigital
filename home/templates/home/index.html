FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions About Digital Marketing in Kolkata

  • What does a digital marketing agency do?

    A digital marketing agency plans and runs a business's online growth activities, including SEO, paid advertising, social media marketing, content creation, and web development, so the business can attract more qualified visitors and convert them into customers. Rizen Digital handles this end-to-end for clients in Kolkata and across India.

  • How much does SEO cost in Kolkata?

    SEO pricing in Kolkata typically depends on the competitiveness of your industry, the number of target keywords, and whether the work includes technical SEO, content, and link building. Rizen Digital offers budget-friendly SEO packages tailored to small and mid-sized businesses; contact us for a free quote based on your specific goals.

  • How long does SEO take to show results?

    Most businesses start seeing measurable SEO improvements within 3 to 6 months, with stronger results compounding over 6 to 12 months. Timelines depend on your website's current state, competition, and how consistently the strategy is executed.

  • What digital marketing services does Rizen Digital offer?

    Rizen Digital offers SEO, social media marketing (SMO), creative branding (logo design, motion graphics, digital media creatives), and full-stack web development, giving clients a single agency for both marketing strategy and technical execution.

  • Is digital marketing worth it for a small business in Kolkata?

    Yes. Digital marketing lets small businesses compete with larger players at a fraction of traditional advertising costs, by targeting local customers actively searching for their products or services online. A focused local SEO and social media strategy is often enough to generate a steady stream of qualified leads.

  • How do I choose the right digital marketing agency in Kolkata?

    Look for an agency with a transparent process, clear reporting, relevant case studies, and services that match your actual goals rather than one-size-fits-all packages. Rizen Digital works closely with each client to build a strategy around their specific budget and industry.
