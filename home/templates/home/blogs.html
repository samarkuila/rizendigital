What you will find on the Rizen Digital blog

We write for business owners and marketing teams who want to understand search and digital marketing well enough to make good decisions, not just approve invoices. Each article is written by our Kolkata team from the work we do with clients: what we test, what we see in Search Console and analytics, and what we would change if we were in your position.

Topics we cover

  • SEO fundamentals – keyword research, on-page optimisation, technical health, and how to build authority without shortcuts.
  • Local search – Google Business Profile, reviews, and location pages for businesses that serve customers in specific cities.
  • Content strategy – planning pages around customer questions, refreshing older content, and measuring what actually brings enquiries.
  • Google updates – plain-language explanations of core and spam updates. See our interactive Google updates guide.
  • AI in marketing – how our AI agents speed up research and reporting while people stay accountable for quality.

How to use these articles

Start with the topic closest to your current problem, then follow the internal links to related guides. If you would rather have someone apply the advice to your own website, talk to our team and we will review your situation and suggest a practical first step.
