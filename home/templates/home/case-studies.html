How we approach client work

Every engagement starts with a diagnosis: where the website stands today, what customers are searching for, and what competitors are doing well. We then agree on a small number of priorities, put a monthly plan behind them, and report progress in plain language. A case study on this page follows the same order: the challenge, what we did, and what changed.

What to look for in a case study

  • The starting point – the real problem the client faced, not a generic goal.
  • The actions – the specific pages, fixes, and content that were changed, so you can judge whether the approach fits your business.
  • The outcome – measurable movement in traffic, enquiries, or visibility, and how long it took.

Results depend on the market, the starting position, and consistent effort, so we avoid promising identical outcomes. What we can promise is a clear process and honest reporting. Curious what that could look like for you? Request a free consultation or explore our services.
