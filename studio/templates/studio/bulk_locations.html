2. Which cities?

One per line: City | Country | local note | latitude | longitude. Only the city is required (country defaults to India). Comma format also works: City, Country, note.

Add a local note for every city. Pages that only swap the city name are thin, near-duplicate content, which Google’s spam policies target. One real, local sentence per city makes each page genuinely useful.
